Research profile

I am interested in how nanoscale physical systems can be understood, modelled and translated into useful technologies.

My research interests span quantum optics, nanophotonics, spintronic devices, quantum transport and computational modelling. A long-term direction is the development of biomedical tools based on nanobio-optoelectronic prototypes, including questions around photon transport in biological tissues.

CURRENT THEMES
  • Quantum and optical phenomena
  • Numerical simulation and modelling
  • Device physics and hardware security
  • Prototype-oriented biomedical photonics

Research approach

I value a workflow that connects physical intuition, mathematical formulation, numerical implementation and experimentally relevant design constraints. This site distinguishes published results from work in progress and makes individual contributions explicit.
